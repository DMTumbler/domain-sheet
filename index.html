Obstacles

Pick a named Obstacle (p.29-37) or Custom. Level defaults to 1d6+2. Roll needed = 12 − value mod + (level ×1 matched, ×2 wrong unit, ×3 wrong both). Hit: level −1d4. Miss: unit gains 1 Disruption. Regrows 1 level/turn if ignored.
Location Obstacle Level Unit sent Match Roll needed Draining held loc. Notes
Unresolved in an unheld location: blocks conquest. Draining a held one: check the box for a −(level÷2) upkeep penalty, auto-added above.

Treasury

1 pt = +1 to a roll (max 5 spent before the roll). After the roll, 2 pts = +1 (max 4 spent). Withdraw: 1st pt = 500gp × Wealth, each further pt in the same action costs one more Treasure than the last.
Separate from Treasure — free, no roll: 0 gp/turn (100gp × Wealth), splittable among officials, convertible to gold.

Atrocity

No penalty. A domain with any Atrocity suffers a −2 upkeep penalty to Military, Wealth, and Social; the penalty worsens by 1 for every full 4 points.
